📘 Clock-hand secrets

A few quick clues can make reading a clock much easier.

1

Find the short hand

The short hand tells the hour. If it sits between 4 and 5, the hour is 4 until it reaches 5.

2

Follow the long hand

The long hand tells the minutes. Start at 12 and count by fives: 5, 10, 15, 20… all the way around.

3

Know your landmarks

12 means :00, 3 means :15, 6 means :30, and 9 means :45.

4

Work out elapsed time

Count forward from the start time to the end time. You can jump to the next hour, then add the remaining minutes.
